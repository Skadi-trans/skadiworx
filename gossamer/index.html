Gossamer

See the changes that happen too slowly to see.

A private transition progress companion for recording the moments, measurements, photos, and milestones that make up your journey—without turning your transition into a social feed.

BUILT FOR THE CHANGES BETWEEN THE MIRROR CHECKS

Track what matters to you.

Gossamer brings the pieces of transition progress into one quiet, private place. Use the parts that help you and leave the rest alone.

◫

Progress Photos

Build a private visual timeline and compare photos side by side when day-to-day changes are hard to notice.

↔

Measurements

Record body measurements over time with built-in guidance designed to help keep each check-in consistent.

✦

Milestones

Save the moments that mattered—from subtle firsts to the days you know you'll want to remember.

△

Progress Comparison

Choose two points in your journey and see how your recorded measurements changed between them.

✓

Medication History

Keep a simple personal record of medication doses you've logged, without treatment recommendations or medical interpretation.

◇

Private Sync

Keep your journey available across your devices with user-controlled cloud sync and private photo storage.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your transition isn't our data. It's yours.

Gossamer is being built around user control: local-first records, private account-based sync, private photo storage, backup and export tools, and account deletion controls.

There is no public profile requirement and no social feed. Your journey is yours to record—not ours to turn into content.

THE GOSSAMER PRINCIPLES

Private by design

Privacy is part of the product architecture, not an afterthought.

User controlled

You decide what Gossamer tracks and what belongs in your journey.

Records, not advice

Gossamer tracks what happened. It doesn't tell you what should happen.
